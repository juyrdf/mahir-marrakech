ScamShield Dashboard

Local guidance & safety tools to avoid traps

🚕
Taxis without Meters
Always demand using the meter or agree on the price beforehand. Fair rate from airport to Medina is between 50-80 MAD.
👤
Fake Guides
Avoid locals proposing to guide you in the alleys for free; they will insist on hefty fees later. Ask Sahbi or use Google Maps.
🐍
Animal Photos (Jemaa el-Fna)
Taking photos with monkeys or snakes costs 100-200 MAD. Confirm the cost first, or they will make a scene.
🧶
Forced Souvenirs
If a merchant forcefully places something in your hand, drop it immediately. This is an aggressive selling technique.

Trusted Spots
